01 / Overview

A different target for each positive pair.

Audio captions differ in specificity, yet standard contrastive training applies the same alignment pressure to every matched audio–caption pair. Dynamic Margin CLAP uses the normalised inverse document frequency of caption tokens to assign a training-time positive-pair margin. More specific descriptions receive a stronger alignment requirement; the margin is removed at inference.

We evaluate the same margin across Euclidean and two Lorentz-hyperbolic embeddings on WavCaps–FreeSound, Clotho, and MACS. The margin improves retrieval consistently. The additional benefit of hyperbolic geometry depends on the corpus.

How to read the geometry result

On WavCaps–FreeSound, the best hyperbolic configuration with the margin exceeds the Euclidean counterpart. On Clotho and MACS, the best hyperbolic and Euclidean margin variants are effectively tied. Curvature also adds retrieval cost, so it is a corpus-dependent choice.
